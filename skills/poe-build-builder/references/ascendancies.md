# Ascendancy Classes — Complete Reference

PoE1 has 7 base classes, each with 3 ascendancy options (except Scion with 2), for 20 total ascendancies. Ascendancy points are earned by completing the Labyrinth (2 points per difficulty: Normal, Cruel, Merciless, Eternal = 8 points max).

---

## MARAUDER (Strength)
Starting area: Top-left of tree. Life, armour, melee, fire.

### Juggernaut — The Immovable Tank
**Identity**: Maximum physical damage reduction and unstoppable momentum.
**Key Nodes:** Unflinching (permanent max endurance charges), Unbreakable (doubled body armour, life regen per endurance charge), Unstoppable (cannot be slowed/frozen/stunned — one of the best single defensive nodes in the game), Unyielding (damage + AoE per endurance charge).
**Best For**: Tanky melee (Earthquake, Cyclone), RF builds, Lab farming. Virtually unkillable.
**Strengths**: Unmatched physical tankiness, stun/freeze immunity. **Weaknesses**: Low offensive scaling.

### Berserker — Maximum Aggression (Reworked 3.25)
**Identity**: Trading safety for raw damage via Rage and self-damage mechanics.
**Key Nodes:** Crave the Slaughter (Rage on hit, damage/speed per Rage), Rite of Ruin (Rage effects tripled, degen tripled — transforms Rage into core mechanic), Aspect of Carnage (40% more damage, 10% increased damage taken — one of highest single-node multipliers in the game), War Bringer (warcries grant Rage, exert attacks), Flawless Savagery (crit multi, flat phys).
**Best For**: Extreme DPS attack builds, slam builds with warcry rotation, any build willing to be fragile for maximum output.
**Strengths**: Highest raw damage multipliers of any ascendancy. **Weaknesses**: Squishy — 10% increased damage taken + Rage degen.

### Chieftain — Fire and Totems
**Identity**: Fire conversion, totem synergy, and endurance charge utility.
**Key Nodes:** Ngamahu (phys as extra fire, Covered in Ash), Hinekora (fire leech, fire pen), Tawhoa (totem phys as extra fire, totem leech to you), Valako (endurance charges via warcries, fire per charge), Ramako (ignite chance, fire damage).
**Best For**: Fire conversion melee (Consecrated Path, Tectonic Slam), totem builds, Righteous Fire.
**Strengths**: Built-in phys-to-fire conversion, totem synergy. **Weaknesses**: Niche — only works with fire/totem builds.

## RANGER (Dexterity)
Starting area: Bottom-right of tree. Evasion, speed, projectiles, flask.

### Deadeye — Projectile Specialist
**Identity**: Projectile manipulation, far shot, and tailwind.
**Key Nodes:** Gathering Winds (Tailwind on kill — +8% action speed, stackable, incredibly powerful), Focal Point (doubled mark effect), Endless Munitions (+2 projectiles with no damage penalty — best projectile node), Far Shot (up to 60% more damage at distance), Ricochet (+1 chain), Occupying Force (+4 mirage archers).
**Best For**: Bow builds (Tornado Shot, Lightning Arrow, Ice Shot), projectile spell builds.
**Strengths**: Free projectiles, tailwind, far shot. Best projectile ascendancy. **Weaknesses**: Almost zero defensive nodes. Glass cannon.

### Raider — Speed and Elemental Avoidance
**Identity**: Permanent Onslaught, Frenzy charges, elemental ailment immunity.
**Key Nodes:** Way of the Poacher (frenzy on hit — works on bosses), Avatar of the Slaughter (more evasion/speed/attack per frenzy), Rapid Assault (permanent Onslaught), Avatar of the Chase (Onslaught enhances evasion + movement + dodge), Avatar of the Veil (elemental ailment immunity, exposure on hit).
**Best For**: Fast mapping (Lightning Strike, Flicker Strike), frenzy stackers, elemental attack builds.
**Strengths**: Permanent onslaught, boss frenzy charges, ailment immunity, very fast. **Weaknesses**: Less raw damage than Deadeye for projectiles.

### Pathfinder — Flask Mastery
**Identity**: Permanent flask uptime, poison, nature utility.
**Key Nodes:** Nature's Reprisal (poison spread on kill), Nature's Boon (flask charges over time — sustain even on bosses, chance not to consume charges), Master Alchemist (ailment immunity during flask effect), Master Surgeon (life flask charges on crit), Master Toxicist (poison damage, flask effect).
**Best For**: Poison builds (Poisonous Concoction, Scourge Arrow), flask-centric builds, magic find.
**Strengths**: Permanent flask uptime trivializes flask management. Ailment immunity. **Weaknesses**: Power tied to flask uptime.

## WITCH (Intelligence)
Starting area: Top-right of tree. Energy shield, spells, minions, curses.

### Necromancer — Minion Master
**Identity**: The definitive summoner ascendancy. Offering skills, minion scaling, corpse manipulation.
**Key Nodes:** Mindless Aggression (minion speed + more damage), Unnatural Strength (+2 to all raise/summon minion gem levels — massive), Bone Barrier (guard skill from minion life, minion life/res), Mistress of Sacrifice (offerings affect you at 50% — Bone Offering = block, Flesh Offering = speed), Plaguebringer (damage + AoE near corpses), Corpse Pact (speed per corpse consumed).
**Best For**: ALL minion builds (Zombies, Skeleton Mages, Spectres, SRS, Absolution, Holy Relic). Also Volatile Dead/Detonate Dead via Corpse Pact.
**Strengths**: Unmatched minion scaling. Offerings affecting you is unique. **Weaknesses**: Mostly locked into minion or corpse builds.

### Elementalist — Elemental Damage and Golems
**Identity**: Elemental damage, ailment application, golem synergy, exposure.
**Key Nodes:** Shaper of Storms (ALL hits shock with min 15% effect), Shaper of Flames (ALL hits ignite regardless of damage type), Shaper of Winter (ALL hits chill/freeze), Heart of Destruction (more ele damage + AoE), Mastermind of Discord (-25% exposure from heralds), Liege of the Primordial (+1 golem, golem buffs doubled), Elemancer (+1 golem, ailment avoidance per golem), Bastion of Elements (Primal Aegis — ele damage shield).
**Best For**: Ignite builds (any element via Shaper of Flames), shock builds, golem builds, herald stacking.
**Strengths**: Shaper nodes enable off-type ailments. Best-in-class exposure. Very flexible. **Weaknesses**: No inherent defense except Bastion. Must choose ailment vs golem path.

### Occultist — Chaos, Curses, and Energy Shield
**Identity**: Chaos damage, curse specialization, ES, enemy debuffs.
**Key Nodes:** Profane Bloom (cursed enemies explode for 25% life as chaos — insane clear), Malediction (+1 curse, enemies cursed take more/deal less damage), Void Beacon (-20% chaos res to nearby enemies), Withering Presence (Wither aura, +60% chaos DoT multi), Vile Bastion (ES regen on kill, stun immunity with ES), Frigid Wake (cold DoT multi, chill immunity, enemy chill).
**Best For**: Chaos DoT (ED/Contagion, Bane, Soulrend), curse builds, cold DoT (Vortex), ES builds.
**Strengths**: Profane Bloom explosions are incredible. Best chaos DoT ascendancy. +1 curse. **Weaknesses**: Offensive nodes mostly DoT-focused.

## DUELIST (Strength/Dexterity)
Starting area: Bottom of tree. Attack, leech, impale, block.

### Slayer — Leech and Overkill
**Identity**: Overleech, cull, massive melee damage.
**Key Nodes:** Endless Hunger (life leech doesn't stop at full life = "overleech," stun/bleed immune while leeching), Brutal Fervour (increased leech rate, leech can't be removed), Headsman (20% cull — enemies below 20% life instantly die), Bane of Legends (20% more damage vs unique, onslaught on kill), Overwhelm (base crit at least 8% for attacks), Impact (+2 weapon range, AoE).
**Best For**: Melee attacks (Cyclone, Bladestorm, Flicker Strike), boss killers (20% cull).
**Strengths**: Overleech is permanent health buffer. 20% cull removes 20% of boss HP. **Weaknesses**: Primarily melee. Relies on leeching, not mitigation.

### Gladiator — Block and Bleed
**Identity**: Maximum block, bleed, on-block effects.
**Key Nodes:** Painforged (block chance, damage on block), Violent Retaliation (phys damage + armour per recent block), Versatile Combatant (max spell block = max attack block — enables spell block capping), Arena Challenger (challenger charges in stances), Blood in the Eyes (blind + maim on hit), Gratuitous Violence (bleeding enemies explode for 10% max life as physical).
**Best For**: Block melee builds, bleed builds (Lacerate, Bladestorm), shield characters.
**Strengths**: Spell block capping. Bleed explosions for clear. Very tanky. **Weaknesses**: Block needs gear investment. Bleed damage ceiling is lower.

### Champion — Impale, Fortify, and Auras
**Identity**: Permanent Fortify, impale mastery, party support.
**Key Nodes:** Fortitude (permanent max Fortification — 20% less damage from hits, always on), Unstoppable Hero (armour/evasion/stun immunity while Fortified), Master of Metal (impale on hit, +2 max impale stacks = 7 total, flat phys per impale — THE impale node), First to Strike (Intimidate on hit + Adrenaline at low life), Inspirational (aura effect, reduced reservation), Conqueror (taunt utility).
**Best For**: Physical attacks (Cyclone, Steel skills), impale builds (best impale class), tanky league starters, party support.
**Strengths**: Permanent Fortify is insane free defense. Impale provides highest sustained phys DPS. **Weaknesses**: Less offensive ceiling for non-impale. Primarily physical.

## TEMPLAR (Strength/Intelligence)
Starting area: Top of tree. Spells, auras, elemental, minions.

### Inquisitor — Elemental Penetration and Consecrated Ground
**Identity**: Ignore enemy resistances on crit, Consecrated Ground sustain.
**Key Nodes:** Inevitable Judgement (crits ignore enemy ele res entirely — equivalent to 40-50% pen against bosses), Righteous Providence (crit chance from Str vs Int), Augury of Penitence (enemies deal less ele damage, take more), Sanctuary + Pious Path (Consecrated Ground: life regen, ES regen = life regen, ailment immunity), Instruments of Virtue/Zeal (attack/spell alternation bonuses, Fanaticism), Battlemage (weapon damage added to spells).
**Best For**: Crit elemental spells, RF (Consecrated Ground regen), CoC builds, hybrid attack/spell.
**Strengths**: Inevitable Judgement is best single offensive crit node for elemental. Pious Path = sustain + ailment immunity. **Weaknesses**: Must build around crit for full value.

### Hierophant — Mana, Totems, and Brands
**Identity**: Massive mana, totem specialization, brand enhancement.
**Key Nodes:** Pursuit of Faith + Ritual of Awakening (+1 totem each, totem damage per active totem — most totems possible), Conviction of Power (minimum 4 power + 4 endurance charges — permanent, no generation needed), Divine Guidance (10% MoM from ascendancy, mana scaling, transfiguration of mind), Illuminated Devotion + Sign of Purpose (brand enhancement), Arcane Blessing (permanent Arcane Surge).
**Best For**: Totem builds (Freezing Pulse totems, Holy Flame, Ballistas), brand builds, mana/Archmage builds.
**Strengths**: Best totem ascendancy. Free permanent charges. Strong mana scaling. **Weaknesses**: Indirect playstyle (totems/brands). Less value for self-cast.

### Guardian — Aura Support and Minion Defense (Buffed 3.28)
**Identity**: Party aura support, minion enhancement, defensive auras.
**Key Nodes:** Radiant Crusade (HoP sentinels + self more damage), Unwavering Crusade (allies/minions more damage + speed), Bastion of Hope (stun immunity, block, spell block), Time of Need (periodic curse/ailment removal, life regen), Harmony of Purpose (share charges with party), Radiant Faith (ES based on reserved life/mana — enables massive ES via aura stacking), Unwavering Faith (armour for you + allies from auras).
**Best For**: Aura-stacking party support, mana guardian (Radiant Faith ES), minion builds, defensive tanks.
**Strengths**: Best party support. Radiant Faith enables huge ES pools. Strong minion synergy. **Weaknesses**: Many nodes are party-focused. Less offensive solo.

## SHADOW (Dexterity/Intelligence)
Starting area: Bottom-left of tree. Crit, traps/mines, DoT, evasion, ES.

### Assassin — Critical Strike Master (Reworked 3.27)
**Identity**: Maximum crit, poison, elusive movement.
**Key Nodes:** Mistwalker (Elusive on crit — avoid damage + movement speed), Opportunistic (more boss damage, reduced damage taken near rares/uniques), Ambush and Assassinate (base crit vs full life, more crit multi, more vs low life), Unstable Infusion (power charge on non-crit) + Deadly Infusion (+2% base crit at max power charges, crit multi per charge), Toxic Delivery + Noxious Strike (poison damage/duration, wither on hit — poison path).
**Best For**: Crit builds (CoC, crit spells, crit attacks), poison builds (BV poison, Venom Gyre).
**Strengths**: Highest crit ceiling. +2% base crit is massive. Elusive for defense. **Weaknesses**: Fragile — evasion + Elusive only.

### Trickster — Hybrid Defense and DoT
**Identity**: Hybrid life/ES, DoT scaling, unique defensive mechanics.
**Key Nodes:** Polymath (damage per different mastery allocated — rewards diverse pathing), Soul Drinker (ES overleech — ES leech doesn't stop at full), Heartstopper (alternating immunity: no DoT / no hit damage, every 2 sec), Escape Artist (body armour evasion → ES, helmet ES → evasion), Swift Killer (frenzy + power on kill), Spellbreaker (spell suppression, ES recovery on suppress), One Step Ahead (cannot be slowed below base, enemies can't exceed base speed).
**Best For**: Hybrid life/ES DoT builds, evasion/ES builds, flexible defensive layering.
**Strengths**: Extremely flexible defensively. Polymath rewards diverse investment. One Step Ahead is unique. **Weaknesses**: No overwhelming offensive node. Jack-of-all-trades.

### Saboteur — Traps and Mines
**Identity**: Trap/mine specialization, blind, regen.
**Key Nodes:** Pyromaniac (life regen per trap/mine triggered, ignite/shock immune while trap/mine active), Born in the Shadows (blind nearby enemies, blinded take more + deal less damage), Demolitions Specialist (mine AoE + damage), Explosives Expert (crit + ele pen for traps/mines), Perfect Crime (trap damage per recent trigger, trap cooldown), Chain Reaction (traps trigger each other — cascade clear).
**Best For**: Mine builds (Ball Lightning mines, Glacial Cascade mines), trap builds (Seismic Trap).
**Strengths**: Safe playstyle (place from range). Good regen + blind defense. **Weaknesses**: Entirely locked into trap/mine. Zero value for other playstyles.

## SCION (All Attributes)
Starting area: Center of tree. Most flexible access.

### Ascendant — Mix-and-Match
**Identity**: Pick two mini-ascendancies from other classes + passive tree start point access.
**Mechanics:** Choose TWO mini-versions of other ascendancies (e.g., mini-Slayer for overleech + cull, mini-Necromancer for +1 spectre + offering effect). Each pick can optionally grant that class's tree starting point — unique to Ascendant. Starting from another area enables efficient pathing to distant nodes.
**Best For**: Aura stackers (multi-start-point), niche combinations, builds needing two specific ascendancy bonuses.
**Strengths**: Most flexible. Starting point access enables unique paths. **Weaknesses**: Mini-versions are weaker than full. Rarely "best in slot."

### Reliquarian — Unique Item Integration (NEW 3.28)
**Identity**: Gain unique item effects without equipping them. Pool rotates per league.
**Mechanics:** Choose one notable from each of three categories (Armour, Weapon [Martial OR Caster path — must pick one], Jewellery). Each notable replicates a famous unique item's signature effect. Gain up to 5 extra passive points (6 if you skip Jewellery). Available pool changes every league. Linear unlock: Armour → Weapon → Jewellery. Most effects stack with the actual unique item. Cannot appear on Forbidden Flesh/Flame.
**3.28 Highlights:** Victario's Influence (+2 aura levels, reservation efficiency), Brass Dome (armour scaling, +max res, crit immunity), Kiloava's Bluster (chance for 90% ele res).
**Best For**: Theorycrafters, builds combining normally-incompatible unique effects. **Strengths**: Unique combinations, extra points. **Weaknesses**: Requires game knowledge, power varies by league.

## Quick Reference: Ascendancy by Role

**Mapping Speed**: Deadeye > Raider > Assassin > Occultist (Profane Bloom)
**Bossing DPS**: Champion (impale) > Berserker (40% more) > Assassin (crit) > Slayer (cull)
**Minions**: Necromancer >>> Guardian > Elementalist (golems)
**Tankiness**: Juggernaut > Champion (fortify) > Gladiator (block) > Necromancer (bone offering)
**DoT/Ailments**: Occultist (chaos) > Elementalist (ignite) > Trickster > Pathfinder (poison)
**Aura Stacking**: Guardian > Ascendant > Champion
**League Start**: Champion > Necromancer > Saboteur > Elementalist

## Decision Framework
1. **Damage type?** Physical → Champion/Slayer/Berserker. Elemental → Inquisitor/Elementalist. Chaos → Occultist/Pathfinder. Minion → Necromancer.
2. **Playstyle?** Melee → Duelist/Marauder. Ranged → Ranger. Caster → Witch/Templar. Totem → Hierophant. Trap/Mine → Saboteur.
3. **Defense priority?** Max tank → Juggernaut/Champion. Leech → Slayer. Block → Gladiator. Evasion → Raider/Trickster. ES → Occultist/Guardian.
4. **Budget?** Low → Champion/Necromancer/Saboteur. High → Berserker/Deadeye/Assassin.
