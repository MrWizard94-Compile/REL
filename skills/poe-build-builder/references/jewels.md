# Jewels — Complete Reference

## Jewel Fundamentals

Jewels are socketable items placed into Jewel Sockets on the passive skill tree. They provide powerful modifiers without taking up gear slots. Jewels are one of the primary endgame scaling systems — a build with well-rolled jewels can have 20-40% more power than one without.

### Socket Locations
- **Tree Jewel Sockets**: Scattered throughout the passive tree. Must allocate the socket node (1 passive point) to use.
- **Large Jewel Sockets**: Outermost ring of the tree — these accept Cluster Jewels only.
- **Abyss Sockets**: Found on specific Abyss-influenced gear (Stygian Vise belt, some uniques). Accept Abyss Jewels only.
- Most builds use 4-7 regular jewel sockets + 1-2 cluster setups

### Jewel Rarity
- **Magic**: 1 prefix + 1 suffix. Cheap, functional early.
- **Rare**: Up to 2 prefixes + 2 suffixes (4 mods total). Standard endgame.
- **Unique**: Fixed mods, often build-defining. Some are extremely powerful.
- Jewels can be corrupted for implicit mods (e.g., "Corrupted Blood cannot be inflicted on you").

## Regular Jewels (Crimson, Viridian, Cobalt)

Three base types, each associated with an attribute area of the tree:
- **Crimson Jewel** (Str/Red): Tends to roll life, physical, melee mods
- **Viridian Jewel** (Dex/Green): Tends to roll evasion, attack speed, projectile mods
- **Cobalt Jewel** (Int/Blue): Tends to roll ES, spell, crit mods
- **Prismatic Jewel**: Can roll mods from all three pools. Used by some uniques.

### What to Look For on Rare Jewels
The "god jewel" for most builds has 4 useful mods. Priority varies by build:

**For Attack Builds:**
- % increased maximum Life (most valuable single mod — 5-7%)
- % increased Attack Speed (with [weapon type] or global)
- % increased Damage (global or weapon-specific)
- % to Critical Strike Multiplier (if crit build)
- Flat added physical damage to attacks

**For Spell Builds:**
- % increased maximum Life or Energy Shield
- % increased Spell Damage or % increased [element] Damage
- % to Critical Strike Multiplier (global or spell-specific)
- % increased Cast Speed
- Flat added damage to spells

**For Minion Builds:**
- % increased Minion Damage
- % increased Minion Life (defensive scaling)
- % increased Minion Attack/Cast Speed
- Minions deal % increased damage if you've used a Minion Skill Recently

**For DoT Builds:**
- % increased maximum Life
- % increased Damage over Time / % Damage over Time Multiplier
- % increased [element] Damage
- % increased Effect of Non-Damaging Ailments (for shock/chill builds)

### Jewel Pricing Rule of Thumb
- 2 good mods: Cheap (1-5c)
- 3 good mods: Moderate (10-50c)
- 4 good mods with high rolls: Expensive (1+ divine)
- Life + Crit Multi + Attack Speed + relevant damage: Premium pricing

## Abyss Jewels

Introduced in the Abyss league. Key differences from regular jewels:
- Can roll FLAT ADDED DAMAGE (not just % increased) — this is their signature strength
- Socket into Abyss Sockets on gear (Stygian Vise belt is the most common source)
- Can also socket into regular tree jewel sockets
- Two base types: **Murderous Eye** (Str), **Searching Eye** (Dex), **Hypnotic Eye** (Int), **Ghastly Eye** (Minion)

### Abyss Jewel Types

**Murderous Eye Jewel** (Str):
- Flat added physical damage to attacks
- Flat added fire damage to attacks
- Life, armour bonuses
- Best for: Physical/fire attack builds

**Searching Eye Jewel** (Dex):
- Flat added cold/lightning damage to attacks
- Accuracy rating
- Life, evasion bonuses
- Best for: Elemental attack builds, bow builds

**Hypnotic Eye Jewel** (Int):
- Flat added damage to SPELLS (unique to Abyss jewels — regular jewels can't roll flat spell damage as easily)
- Energy shield
- Best for: Spell builds, ES builds

**Ghastly Eye Jewel** (Minion):
- Flat added damage to minion attacks/spells
- Minion life, minion attack/cast speed
- "Minions have % chance to Taunt/Blind on hit"
- Best for: Minion builds — THE jewel type for summoners

### Why Abyss Jewels Matter
- Flat added damage bypasses the "increased damage" diminishing returns problem
- On builds with high "damage effectiveness" skills, flat damage from Abyss jewels is extremely efficient
- Stacking 5+ Abyss jewels (via Abyss socket gear + tree sockets) with flat damage can be a core build strategy
- Ghastly Eye jewels with "Minions have flat added [element] damage" directly buff all your minions

### Stygian Vise
- Belt base type with an Abyss socket (only belt that can socket a jewel)
- Strictly better than any other rare belt base if the Abyss jewel is good
- Standard endgame belt for most builds

## Cluster Jewels

The most complex and powerful jewel system. Cluster Jewels create entirely new branches of the passive tree.

### How They Work
1. Socket a **Large Cluster Jewel** into a Large Jewel Socket (outermost tree ring)
2. This creates a new sub-tree with small passive nodes, notable passives, and Medium Jewel Sockets
3. Socket **Medium Cluster Jewels** into those Medium sockets
4. Medium clusters create more nodes + a Small Jewel Socket
5. Socket **Small Cluster Jewels** or regular jewels into the Small sockets

### Size Tiers

**Large Cluster Jewels:**
- 8-12 passive skills added
- 2 Medium Jewel Sockets
- Up to 3 notable passives (2 prefix, 1 suffix)
- **Optimal: 8 passives** — fewest travel nodes to reach notables + sockets
- Drops from Delirium encounters

**Medium Cluster Jewels:**
- 4-6 passive skills added
- 1 Small Jewel Socket
- Up to 2 notable passives (both prefixes)
- **Optimal: 4 or 5 passives** — depends on whether you want both notables
- Drops from Delirium encounters

**Small Cluster Jewels:**
- 2-3 passive skills added
- No sockets
- Up to 1 notable passive (prefix)
- **Optimal: 2 passives** (minimum travel)
- Often used for specific defensive notables or keystones

### Cluster Jewel Enchantments
Each cluster jewel has TWO fixed enchantments (cannot be changed with currency):
1. **"Adds X Passive Skills"** — determines the layout (8 vs 12 passives, etc.)
2. **"Small Passives grant [stat]"** — determines what the travel nodes give AND what notable mods the jewel can roll

The second enchantment is critical — it locks the mod pool. A "12% increased Fire Damage" Large Cluster can only roll fire-related notables. A "12% increased Attack Damage while holding a Shield" cluster rolls shield attack notables.

### Notable Stacking
- Notables from different cluster jewels DO stack with each other
- You CAN have the same notable on multiple clusters — all instances apply
- Some notables have "Limited to 1" — these don't stack (check the notable text)
- This means you can stack powerful notables to absurd levels

### Cluster Jewel Point Efficiency
A typical full cluster setup (Large + 2 Medium + 2 Small/regular):
- Costs ~15-18 passive points total (including the tree socket)
- Grants: 3 Large notables + 4 Medium notables + 2 Small notables/jewels
- This is significantly more efficient than pathing across the tree for equivalent power
- Especially valuable for builds in awkward tree positions

### Key Notable Categories (Examples)
- **Aura effect**: Purposeful Harbinger, Replenishing Presence (Medium, aura clusters)
- **Minion damage**: Renewal, Rotten Claws, Feasting Fiends (Large, minion clusters)
- **DoT**: Wasting Affliction, Brush with Death (Medium, DoT clusters)
- **Crit**: Pressure Points, Quick Getaway (Medium, crit clusters)
- **Life**: Fettle (Small, life clusters — flat +20 max life notable)
- **Mana reservation**: First Among Equals (Medium, aura clusters)

### Crafting Cluster Jewels
- Roll with Alterations/Chaos/Fossils like any item
- Item level matters — higher ilvl = access to more notable mods
- The enchantment determines the mod pool — check poedb.tw for available mods per enchantment
- Harvest crafting can be very effective for targeted notable rolling
- 5-to-1 vendor recipe: Sell 5 same-size same-rarity clusters → get 1 new cluster with new enchantment + mods

## Timeless Jewels

Five faction-specific jewels that fundamentally transform a section of the passive tree within their radius. Each has a unique mechanic.

### How They Work
- Socket into any regular jewel socket
- All non-keystone passive nodes within the jewel's radius are REPLACED or AUGMENTED with new modifiers
- The specific changes are determined by the jewel's seed number (shown on the jewel)
- Each seed produces a FIXED result — same seed always gives same transformations
- Use tools like poedb.tw or timeless-jewel-search tools to find desired seeds

### The Five Timeless Jewels

**Glorious Vanity** (Vaal — Doryani/Xibaqua/Ahuana):
- Transforms passives in radius into random new modifiers
- Keystones in radius become faction-specific:
  - Doryani: **Corrupted Soul** (50% of non-chaos damage bypasses ES, gain 15% of max life as extra ES)
  - Xibaqua: **Divine Flesh** (all damage bypasses ES, 50% of ele damage taken as chaos, +5% max chaos res)
  - Ahuana: **Immortal Ambition** (ES recharge applies to Life instead — enables overleech-like behavior for ES)
- Highly variable — requires seed-searching for good small node transformations

**Elegant Hubris** (Eternal Empire — Cadiro/Caspiro/Victario):
- ALL passives in radius become "5% increased [various stat]" or blank nodes
- Keystones in radius become faction-specific:
  - Cadiro: **Supreme Decadence** (life flask recovery also applies to ES)
  - Caspiro: **Supreme Ostentation** (ignore attribute requirements)
  - Victario: **Supreme Grandstanding** (taunted enemies deal 10% less to others)

**Lethal Pride** (Karui — Kaom/Rakiata/Kiloava):
- Adds secondary bonuses ON TOP of existing passives (doesn't replace them)
- Possible additions: Strength, fire damage, melee damage, intimidate, double damage chance
- Keystones become:
  - Kaom: **Strength of Blood** (life recovery from flasks applies to ES, life leech replaced with lifetap)
  - Rakiata: **Tempered by the War** (+5% max fire/cold res, -50% lightning res, 50% lightning as fire/cold)
  - Kiloava: **Glancing Blows** (double block chance, but blocked hits deal 65% damage)
- Excellent for Strength-stacking or melee builds

**Brutal Restraint** (Maraketh — Deshret/Asenath/Nasima):
- Adds secondary bonuses ON TOP of existing passives (dexterity-focused)
- Possible additions: Dexterity, evasion, attack speed, projectile damage, onslaught on kill
- Keystones become:
  - Deshret: **Wind Dancer** (40% less attack damage taken if not hit recently, 20% more if hit recently)
  - Asenath: **Dance with Death** (no helmets, +40% crit multi, lucky crits but enemy crits also lucky)
  - Nasima: **Second Sight** (blind immunity, 25% more melee crit while blinded)

**Militant Faith** (Templar — Dominus/Avarius/Maxarius):
- Transforms notables to "Devotion" (generic increased damage), small passives gain bonuses
- Keystones become:
  - Dominus: **Inner Conviction** (3% more spell damage per power charge, lose frenzy charges)
  - Avarius: **Power of Purpose** (mana provides armour)
  - Maxarius: **Transcendence** (armour applies to elemental damage instead of physical, -15% max phys reduction)

### Practical Timeless Jewel Usage
1. Decide which keystone transformation you want (if any)
2. Use seed-search tools to find seeds with good small node transformations
3. Timeless jewels are limited to 1 equipped per character
4. Best setups provide both a strong keystone AND useful small node additions

## Key Unique Jewels

### Build-Defining

**Watcher's Eye** (Prismatic): 2-3 aura-conditional mods. THE endgame jewel. See auras-reservation.md.

**Thread of Hope** (Crimson): Allocate passives in a ring without connecting. -10 to -20% all res downside. Saves 5-10 passive points. Small/Medium/Large/Very Large variants.

**Forbidden Flesh / Forbidden Flame** (Cobalt/Crimson pair): Matching pair grants an extra ascendancy notable. Effectively a 5th ascendancy allocation.

**Impossible Escape** (Viridian): Passives near a named keystone can be allocated without connection.

**Unnatural Instinct** (Viridian): Auto-allocates all small passives in radius. Best in Scion area.

**Sublime Vision** (Prismatic): Disables all but one aura, massively enhances it. Uber Maven drop.

### Utility Uniques

**Energy From Within** (Cobalt): Life increases in radius apply to ES instead. Core for ES builds near life clusters.

**Might of the Meek** (Crimson): 50% increased small passive effect, notables grant nothing. Best in Scion area.

**Split Personality** (Prismatic): +1 to two stats per passive between it and class start. Scales with distance.

**Anatomical Knowledge / Brute Force / Fertile Mind / Inertia**: Convert one attribute's nodes to another within radius.

## Corruption Implicits on Jewels

### High-Value Corruptions
- **"Corrupted Blood cannot be inflicted on you"** — THE most valuable. Permanent CB immunity, frees flask suffix.
- **"X% increased Attack/Cast Speed"**
- **"X% increased Effect of Non-Curse Auras"** — valuable for aura builds
- **"X% to all Elemental Resistances"**
- **"X% chance to Avoid being Stunned"**

Strategy: Buy cheap jewels with good mods, corrupt hoping for CB immunity. Even mediocre explicit mods are worth it for this implicit.

## Practical Decision Framework

### Investment Priority
1. Fill all tree sockets with cheap 2-mod jewels (life + damage) — immediate power for 1-5c each
2. Upgrade to 3-4 mod jewels as budget allows
3. Add cluster jewel setup if strong notables exist for your build
4. Stygian Vise + good Abyss jewel for belt slot
5. Timeless jewel if a keystone transformation benefits you
6. Watcher's Eye for aura-specific scaling
7. Thread of Hope / Impossible Escape for passive point savings
8. Forbidden Flesh/Flame pair as endgame luxury
9. Get one "Corrupted Blood cannot be inflicted" jewel — prevents a common death cause

### Common Mistakes
1. Empty jewel sockets — even a 1c jewel beats an empty socket
2. Ignoring Corrupted Blood immunity corruption
3. Overpaying for clusters when tree notables provide similar power
4. Wrong Abyss jewel type for your build
5. Random timeless jewels without checking seeds
6. Forgetting jewel sockets cost a passive point
