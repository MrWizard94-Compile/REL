# Leveling & Campaign — Complete Reference

## Campaign Structure

The PoE1 campaign consists of 10 Acts. Completing all 10 unlocks the endgame Atlas/mapping system. A fast player can complete the campaign in 5-8 hours; a new player typically takes 15-25 hours.

### Act Progression Summary

**Act 1** (Levels 1-12): Lioneye's Watch → Merveil boss. Get your first skill gems from quest rewards. Kill Brutus (Act 1 mid-boss) for additional gem rewards. Merveil drops cold-themed loot.

**Act 2** (Levels 12-20): Forest of the Southern Coast → Vaal Oversoul boss. Major gem reward: Help Eramir for Book of Skill (+1 passive point). Deal with the Bandits quest (see bandits-pantheon.md). Access to most core support gems by end of Act 2.

**Act 3** (Levels 20-28): City of Sarn → Dominus boss. Library quest unlocks purchase of most gem types from Siosa. Fixture of Fate quest gives all remaining gems. Piety fight in Lunaris Temple is a gear/mechanics check.

**Act 4** (Levels 28-36): Highgate → Malachai boss. Normal Labyrinth available (do it around level 33-36 for first ascendancy). Belly of the Beast is a difficulty spike — ensure resistances are capped.

**Act 5** (Levels 36-42): Slave Pens → Kitava boss. **CRITICAL: Killing Act 5 Kitava gives -30% to all resistances permanently.** Fix your resistances before progressing to Act 6.

**Act 6** (Levels 42-48): Lioneye's Watch (again) → Brine King boss. Twilight Strand quest: Lilly Roth now sells ALL gem types. Difficulty spikes — Act 6+ is noticeably harder than Acts 1-5.

**Act 7** (Levels 48-52): Bridge Encampment → Arakaali boss. Cruel Labyrinth available (do around level 55).

**Act 8** (Levels 52-56): Sarn Encampment → Lunaris/Solaris boss. Doedre/Maligaro/Shavronne trilogy.

**Act 9** (Levels 56-60): Highgate → Depraved Trinity boss. Quarry zone is popular for leveling (reset and re-enter to farm).

**Act 10** (Levels 60-68): Oriath → Kitava boss (final). **CRITICAL: Killing Act 10 Kitava gives another -30% resistances (total -60% from both Kitavas).** After completion, the Atlas opens. Aim to be level 65-70 when entering maps.

## Leveling Skill Recommendations

Most builds DON'T use their final skill during the campaign. Use strong leveling skills, then transition.

### Universal Leveling Skills (Work for Any Class)

**Freezing Pulse** (Level 1): Available immediately. Strong cold spell with built-in freeze. Works until Act 3-4 when you transition.

**Stormblast Mine** (Level 1): Very strong for any intelligence class. Mines are safe (place and detonate from range). Transitions into any mine build or swap at Act 3.

**Splitting Steel** (Level 1): Strong for any strength/dexterity class. Good single target + clear with Call of Steel.

**Caustic Arrow** (Level 1): DoT-based, great for ranger. Scales with gem level, doesn't need weapon DPS.

**Holy Flame Totem** (Level 4): Safe leveling — place totems, stay at range. Works for any Templar or totem-adjacent build.

**Absolution** (Level 12): Minion-based spell. Creates temporary sentinels on kill/hit vs rares. Very strong once you get enough hits.

### Class-Specific Leveling Paths

**Marauder/Duelist (Melee):**
- Acts 1-3: Splitting Steel or Ground Slam → Sunder when available
- Acts 3-6: Transition to main skill (Cyclone at 28, Earthquake, Bladestorm)
- Support: Added Fire, Melee Physical, Faster Attacks

**Ranger (Bow/Attack):**
- Acts 1-2: Caustic Arrow or Splitting Steel
- Acts 3+: Rain of Arrows, Toxic Rain (level 12+), or Lightning Arrow
- Support: Mirage Archer, Added Cold/Lightning, Pierce

**Witch (Spell/Minion):**
- Acts 1-3: Freezing Pulse → Arc or Essence Drain at Act 3
- Minion path: SRS (level 4) → Summon Skeletons (level 10) → Absolution (12) → Raise Zombie/Spectre
- Support: Added Lightning/Cold, Spell Echo, Minion Damage

**Shadow (Crit/Trap/DoT):**
- Acts 1-3: Stormblast Mine → Pyroclast/Icicle Mine or Toxic Rain
- Poison path: Caustic Arrow → Poisonous Concoction at Act 3
- Support: Trap and Mine Damage, Added Cold, Swift Assembly

**Templar (Spell/Totem):**
- Acts 1-3: Freezing Pulse or Holy Flame Totem → Storm Brand or Armageddon Brand
- Support: Added Lightning, Elemental Focus (if not needing ailments), Concentrated Effect

**Scion:**
- Most flexible — use whatever feels good from quest rewards
- SRS or Absolution early → transition to build-specific skill by Act 4

## Gear Progression During Campaign

### Gear Priority by Act

**Acts 1-4 (Everything is temporary):**
- Pick up and identify ALL rares — look for life + elemental resistances
- Weapon: Biggest DPS weapon you find (for attacks) or +spell damage wand/sceptre (for spells)
- Links: Get a 3-link by Act 2, 4-link by Act 4
- Movement speed boots: Huge quality of life — even 10% is noticeable
- Don't waste currency on crafting — everything gets replaced

**Acts 5-6 (Post-first Kitava):**
- **FIX RESISTANCES** — you just lost 30%, need to re-cap at 75%
- Upgrade to better life + resistance rares
- 4-link is mandatory by now
- Start looking for a 5-link body armour (can buy one cheaply from vendors or other players)

**Acts 7-10 (Push to maps):**
- Continue prioritizing life + resistances
- **After Act 10 Kitava**: Need 135%+ of each elemental resistance on gear to cap at 75% after the -60% penalty
- Weapon upgrade matters more now — look for better base type
- Start thinking about your build's specific unique items
- 5-link body armour is strongly recommended

### Vendor Recipes for Leveling

**+1 to Level of [Element] Gems wand/sceptre:**
- Magic wand/sceptre + Alteration Orb + gem of the desired element
- Result: Weapon with "+1 to Level of All [Element] Spell Skill Gems"
- EXTREMELY powerful for spell leveling — use from Act 1

**Movement speed boots:**
- Magic boots + Augmentation Orb + Quicksilver Flask
- Result: Boots with movement speed
- Nice quality of life if you haven't found MS boots naturally

**Chromatic Orb:**
- Vendor any item with linked Red + Green + Blue sockets
- Easy way to get chromes early

**Chaos Orb Recipe:**
- Full set of rare items (each slot filled) at ilvl 60-74 → 1 Chaos Orb
- All unidentified → 2 Chaos Orbs
- Start collecting full sets in Act 9-10 for early map currency

## Passive Point Quests

These quests award passive skill points or skill refund points. **Do not skip these.**

### Passive Point Quests by Act (22-24 total)

**Act 1:** The Marooned Mariner (+1 point), The Way Forward (+1 point)
**Act 2:** Through Sacred Ground (+1 point), Deal with the Bandits (+2 points if you kill all, or +1 point + bandit bonus)
**Act 3:** Victario's Secrets (+1 point), Piety's Pets (+1 point)
**Act 4:** An Indomitable Spirit (+1 point)
**Act 5:** In Service to Science (+1 point), Kitava's Torments (+2 points)
**Act 6:** The Father of War (+1 point), The Puppet Mistress (+1 point)
**Act 7:** The Master of a Million Faces (+1 point), Queen of Despair (+1 point), Kishara's Star (+1 point)
**Act 8:** Love is Dead (+1 point), Reflection of Terror (+1 point)
**Act 9:** Queen of the Sands (+1 point), The Storm Blade (+1 point)
**Act 10:** Vilenta's Vengeance (+1 point), An End to Hunger (+2 points)

### Skill Refund Point Quests (~20 total)
Various quests throughout the campaign grant refund points instead of passive points. These accumulate and can be used to respec individual passive nodes.

## Labyrinth Progression

### Four Lab Difficulties

**Normal Lab** (Level ~33): 2 ascendancy points. Do in Act 3-4. Requires completing 6 trials in Acts 1-3.
**Cruel Lab** (Level ~55): 2 more ascendancy points. Do in Act 7-8. Requires 6 trials in Acts 6-7.
**Merciless Lab** (Level ~68): 2 more ascendancy points. Do in early maps. Requires 6 trials found randomly in maps.
**Eternal Lab** (Level ~75+): 2 final ascendancy points. Requires Offering to the Goddess (drops from map trials). Hardest version — Izaro hits very hard.

### Ascendancy Point Priority
- Your FIRST 2 points should go to the most impactful node for your build
- Plan which nodes you take at each lab difficulty — order matters
- Example: Necromancer typically takes Mindless Aggression first, then Unnatural Strength
- Example: Champion takes Fortitude first (permanent Fortify is an immediate massive defensive boost)

## Transition Points — When to Switch Skills

### Common Transition Points

**Level 1-12**: Use whatever gem is available and strong. Freezing Pulse, Stormblast Mine, Splitting Steel.

**Level 12-16**: Most builds can start their leveling skill here. Absolution, Toxic Rain, Storm Brand, SRS.

**Level 28**: Major transition point — many powerful skills unlock (Cyclone, Blade Vortex, Summon Skeletons with Dead Reckoning).

**Level 38**: Another wave of skills — Cast on Crit, Spell Echo, and many endgame-ready support gems.

**Act 6 (Lilly Roth)**: ALL gems become purchasable. If you need any gem you missed, buy it here.

**Early Maps (Level 68-75)**: Final transition to endgame setup. Switch to your actual main skill + 5/6-link.

### Signs You Need to Transition
- Your leveling skill starts feeling slow (kills take too long)
- You have the gem level and links for your main skill
- Your ascendancy specifically synergizes with the new skill
- You found/bought the unique item that enables your build

## Speed Leveling Tips

### Movement is King
- Keep a Quicksilver Flask active at all times
- Use a movement skill (Flame Dash, Leap Slam, Shield Charge) from Act 1 onward
- Don't full-clear zones — push forward, kill enough to stay within 2-3 levels of the zone
- Know the zone layouts — most zones have consistent general directions
- Waypoints are your checkpoints — grab them, portal to town for quest turn-ins

### Damage Keeps You Moving
- Keep your main skill gem leveled — click the "+" when it appears
- Keep your weapon updated — vendor recipe for +1 gem level wand is huge for spells
- Links matter more than base item quality — a 4-link with bad base beats a 0-link with good base
- Added damage supports (Added Fire, Added Cold, Added Lightning) are strong during leveling

### Survival Basics
- **Life on every piece of gear** — even +20 life matters in Act 1
- **Resistance capping after each Kitava** — non-negotiable, you will die without it
- **One life flask with instant or bubbling prefix** — panic button
- **Don't overlevel** — if you're 5+ levels above the zone, you're wasting time. Push forward.
- **Don't die in Acts** — each death costs 5% (softcore) or is permanent (hardcore). Staying alive is faster than respawning.

### Efficiency Tricks
- **Vendor recipe weapons early**: +1 gem level wands/sceptres from Act 1
- **Buy gems from vendors**: Don't wait for drops — buy the gems you need
- **Skip optional content**: Side quests that don't give passive points can wait
- **Use portal scrolls liberally**: Portal to town to sell/stash, come back. Don't carry a full inventory.
- **Logout macro** (HC): A macro that force-closes the client. Mandatory for hardcore — it's faster than portaling when in danger.

## Common Leveling Mistakes

1. **Full-clearing every zone**: Only kill what's in your path. You need level parity, not over-leveling.
2. **Ignoring resistances after Kitava**: The #1 death cause for new players. Fix res immediately.
3. **Not doing lab**: Ascendancy points are the single biggest power spikes. Do them ASAP.
4. **Using endgame skill too early**: Some skills are terrible at low gem levels or without proper supports. Use a leveling skill, then transition.
5. **Spending currency during leveling**: Save your Chaos, Alchs, and anything valuable. You'll need them in maps.
6. **Not buying gems from vendors**: You don't need to wait for gem drops. Buy from Lilly (Act 6) or Siosa (Act 3 Library).
7. **Trying to craft leveling gear**: Everything you wear during the campaign gets replaced. Don't waste resources on it.
8. **Skipping passive point quests**: Every passive point matters. Complete all point-granting quests.
9. **Not using a loot filter**: Install NeverSink's loot filter before you start. Without one, you'll waste time identifying junk.
10. **Playing without a build plan**: The passive tree is permanent (respeccing costs currency). Follow a guide or plan your tree before spending points.

## Leveling-to-Maps Checklist

Before entering your first map, verify:

- [ ] All 10 Acts completed
- [ ] Both Kitava resistance penalties accounted for (need 135%+ each ele res on gear)
- [ ] At least 3500-4000 life (softcore) or 4500+ (hardcore)
- [ ] Normal + Cruel Lab completed (4 ascendancy points)
- [ ] Main skill in a 4-link minimum (5-link preferred)
- [ ] All passive point quests completed (check the quest tracker)
- [ ] Loot filter installed and updated
- [ ] Movement skill + guard skill equipped
- [ ] Flasks crafted with bleed removal (of Staunching) and freeze removal (of Heat) at minimum
- [ ] Basic understanding of map mods (which mods your build can't run)
