# Endgame Content — Complete Reference

## Atlas of Worlds Overview (Reworked in 3.28)

After completing the 10-act campaign, the endgame opens via the Atlas of Worlds — a massive interconnected map system where you run progressively harder content, fight pinnacle bosses, and farm currency/items.

### 3.28 Atlas Structure
The Atlas has been fundamentally reworked in 3.28 Mirage:

**Generic Tiered Maps**: Maps no longer drop as specific layouts (e.g., "Tier 10 Dunes"). Instead, you find a "Tier 10 Map" and choose ANY layout you've unlocked on the Atlas. This eliminates map sustain frustrations and allows instant strategy switching.

**Center-Start Layout**: You begin at the center of the Atlas and expand outward. Four quadrants extend toward the corners, each containing a Voidstone slot tied to a specific boss encounter chain.

**Voidstone Quadrants** (each corner houses one):
- **Bottom-Left**: Searing Exarch + Eater of Worlds Voidstone
- **Bottom-Right**: Maven Voidstone
- **Top-Left**: Eagon/Incarnation Voidstone (new 3.28 boss chain)
- **Top-Right**: Elder + Shaper (Uber Elder) Voidstone

**Voidstone Effects** (updated 3.28):
- Each Voidstone upgrades minimum map tier in its quadrant to T16
- Causes maps in that quadrant to drop one tier higher
- Converts T15 and lower maps to Gold currency
- Enables Nightmare Maps (formerly T17s) to drop
- Each Voidstone also grants a unique bonus affecting all maps

## Map Tiers and Progression

### Map Tier System
- **White Maps**: Tiers 1-5. Easy, introductory. Used for Atlas progression and learning.
- **Yellow Maps**: Tiers 6-10. Moderate difficulty. Maven witnessing begins. Boss influence mechanics start.
- **Red Maps**: Tiers 11-16. Full endgame difficulty. All boss encounters accessible. Scarab juicing matters.
- **Nightmare Maps**: T17+. The hardest regular mapping content. Requires Voidstones. Extreme difficulty + extreme rewards.

### Map Modifiers
Maps can be rolled with modifiers that increase difficulty AND rewards:
- **Normal** (white): No mods. Minimal rewards.
- **Magic** (blue, Transmutation): 1-2 mods. Low-medium difficulty.
- **Rare** (yellow, Alchemy): 4-6 mods. Standard endgame approach ("alch and go").
- **Corrupted** (Vaal Orb): Adds 1 unremovable mod + 8 mods total. Can roll dangerous combinations.

**Dangerous Map Mods to Watch For:**
- "Monsters reflect X% Elemental/Physical Damage" — WILL KILL builds that deal the reflected type. Check before running.
- "Players cannot Regenerate Life, Mana, or Energy Shield" — Kills RF, regen-based builds.
- "Monsters have X% chance to Avoid Ailments" — Weakens ailment-dependent builds.
- "Players have X% less Recovery Rate" — Dangerous for leech/regen builds.
- "-X% maximum Player Resistances" — Makes elemental damage much more lethal.
- "Area has increased Monster Density" — Actually GOOD (more monsters = more loot), but makes the map harder.

### Scarabs (Map Juicing)
Scarabs are consumable items placed in the Map Device alongside your map. They add specific league mechanics or enhance rewards:
- Start with 3 Scarab slots, unlock up to 5 through progression
- Each Scarab type adds a specific mechanic (Breach, Legion, Delirium, Harvest, etc.)
- Higher-tier Scarabs = more/better rewards from that mechanic
- **"Alch and go"** = Alchemy Orb the map + run it. Cheap, efficient for new players.
- **"Juiced"** = Multiple Scarabs + influence + Atlas passives + high-roll map mods. Expensive but extremely rewarding.

### Astrolabes (Replacing Sextants in 3.28)
Astrolabes are new items that create "Shaped Regions" on the Atlas:
- Apply an Astrolabe to a map to influence a region
- Shaped Regions add specific encounters and modifiers to maps within the area
- Completing shaped content strengthens remaining maps in the region
- Finishing an entire Shaped Region grants access to a Memory Vault with powerful rewards
- Memory Vaults include: Archon's Vault (unique items), Zealot's Vault (currency), and others

## Atlas Passive Skill Tree

A separate passive tree for your Atlas that determines which mechanics appear in maps and how rewarding they are.

### Basics
- **138 total points** available in 3.28 (up from 132)
- 100 points from completing Atlas Objectives (magic/rare/corrupted maps)
- 10 points from completing any 10 of the available Unique Maps
- 5 points from completing Nightmare Maps (1 per Nightmare Map completed)
- Remaining points from specific boss encounters and content
- Points can be freely respecced (costs Orbs of Regret)

### Strategy
- **Early mapping**: Invest in map sustain nodes (increased map drops, connected map drops)
- **Mid mapping**: Invest in your preferred mechanic (Breach, Legion, Delirium, Harvest, etc.)
- **Late mapping**: Specialize heavily — stack one or two mechanics for maximum profit
- **Boss farming**: Respec into boss-encounter nodes when running pinnacle content
- The Atlas tree is meant to be RESPECCED frequently based on what you're farming

## Boss Encounter Chains

### Tier 1: Sub-Bosses (Entry Level)

**The Black Star** (Exarch precursor):
- Access: Polaric Invitation (from 14 Exarch-influenced T14+ maps)
- Difficulty: Moderate. Cold-themed attacks, area denial.
- Drops: Polaric-themed items, progression toward Exarch

**The Infinite Hunger** (Eater precursor):
- Access: Writhing Invitation (from 14 Eater-influenced T14+ maps)
- Difficulty: Moderate. Chaos/cold attacks, tentacle mechanics.
- Drops: Eater-themed items, progression toward Eater of Worlds

**Shaper Guardians** (4 bosses: Purifier, Constrictor, Enslaver, Eradicator):
- Access: Maps with "occupied by [Guardian]" modifier (drop from T14+ map bosses)
- Difficulty: Moderate-Hard. Each has unique mechanics.
- Drops: Guardian map fragment (1 of 4 needed for Shaper encounter)

**Elder Guardians** (4 bosses: same as Shaper Guardians but Elder-flavored):
- Access: Maps with Elder Guardian occupation
- Difficulty: Moderate-Hard
- Drops: Elder fragment (1 of 4 needed for Elder encounter)

**Conquerors of the Atlas** (4 bosses: Al-Hezmin, Baran, Drox, Veritania):
- Access: Maps occupied by Conquerors
- Difficulty: Moderate. Each themed around their influence type.
- Drops: Conqueror-influenced items, progression toward Sirus

### Tier 2: Pinnacle Bosses

**The Searing Exarch**:
- Access: Incandescent Invitation (from 28 Exarch-influenced T14+ maps after defeating Black Star)
- Difficulty: Hard. Fire-themed. Famous "rolling meteor wall" phase requires precise dodging.
- Key Mechanics: Flame walls, mortar shots, rolling meteor phases (Maven creates gaps), searing rune ground patterns.
- Drops: Omniscient Voidstone (first kill), Exarch-themed uniques, Eldritch Ember currency, Forbidden Flame jewels.
- Tips: Bring Ruby Flask. Use movement skills to dodge meteors. Minions/totems can block meteor paths.

**The Eater of Worlds**:
- Access: Screaming Invitation (from 28 Eater-influenced T14+ maps after defeating Infinite Hunger)
- Difficulty: Hard. Cold/chaos-themed. Area denial and tentacle attacks.
- Key Mechanics: Tentacle slams, moving AoE zones, cold damage bursts, intermission phases.
- Drops: Grasping Voidstone (first kill), Eater-themed uniques, Eldritch Ichor currency, Forbidden Flesh jewels.
- Tips: Chaos resistance matters here. Movement speed helps dodge tentacles.

**The Shaper**:
- Access: Key to the Crucible (combine 4 Shaper Guardian fragments)
- Difficulty: Hard. One of the original pinnacle fights. Multi-phase with adds.
- Key Mechanics: Beam attack (teleport behind him), slam (dodge to side), bullet hell phases, Zana shield phases, add phases with dangerous ground effects.
- Drops: Fragment of Shape/Knowledge (needed for Uber Elder), Shaper-themed uniques (Starforge, Dying Sun, etc.)
- Tips: Learn the beam telegraph. Stay mobile during bullet hell. Protect Zana during her shield phases.

**The Elder**:
- Access: Key to Decay (combine 4 Elder Guardian fragments)
- Difficulty: Hard. Tentacle/cold themed. Ground degens and positioning.
- Key Mechanics: Ice spear volleys, expanding ring attacks, ground degens, Shaper appears to help/interfere.
- Drops: Fragment of Emptiness/Terror (needed for Uber Elder), Elder-themed uniques (Watcher's Eye from Uber Elder).

**Uber Elder** (Elder + Shaper simultaneously):
- Access: Combine 2 Shaper fragments + 2 Elder fragments
- Difficulty: Very Hard. Both bosses fight you at the same time. Their mechanics overlap.
- Key Mechanics: All Shaper + Elder mechanics happening simultaneously. Massive ground degen coverage. Must manage two boss health bars and both sets of attacks.
- Drops: Watcher's Eye (2-mod version from regular, 3-mod from Uber), Mark of the Shaper/Elder rings, Voidwalker boots.
- Tips: This is a mechanical skill check. Learn each boss's patterns individually first. The fight becomes a dance of dodging both sets of attacks.

**The Maven**:
- Access: Maven's Writ (combine 10 Crescent Splinters from Maven-witnessed boss kills and Invitations)
- Difficulty: Very Hard — considered the hardest standard pinnacle boss.
- Key Mechanics: Memory Game (must walk over specific tiles in order — failure = massive damage), Cascade of Pain, gravity wells that pull you in, tri-beam attacks, intermission phases with previously-witnessed bosses.
- Drops: Maven's Voidstone, Elevated Sextants (legacy), Maven's Orb (for elevating influenced mods), Awakened Support Gems (now limited), unique items.
- Tips: Memory game is the make-or-break mechanic. Practice the sequence. Gravity wells persist — manage arena space carefully.

**Sirus, Awakener of Worlds**:
- Access: Kill all 4 Conquerors → Sirus encounter opens
- Difficulty: Hard. Multi-phase. Famous "Die" beam.
- Key Mechanics: Apparition attacks between phases, corridor phase, "Die" beam (rotate behind him), rotating beams, meteor maze, storm phases that limit arena.
- Drops: Awakener's Orb (combines two influences), Sirus-themed uniques (Hands of the High Templar, Thread of Hope)
- Tips: The "Die" beam is telegraphed — dash BEHIND him when he says it. Don't panic during storm phases.

**Incarnations** (3.28 Eagon questline — new):
- **Incarnation of Neglect**, **Incarnation of Fear**, **Incarnation of Dread**
- Access: Progress through Eagon's Memory-influenced maps in the top-left Atlas quadrant
- Difficulty: Hard to Very Hard (Dread is the final one)
- Drops: Incarnation of Dread drops the Originator Voidstone, which enables Exceptional Support Gem drops from Atlas bosses
- Part of the new 3.28 boss chain

**Cortex (Venarius)**:
- Access: Cortex unique map (drops from various sources)
- Difficulty: Hard. Synthesis-themed. You fight Venarius's creations across a Synthesis map.
- Drops: Synthesised unique items, guaranteed unique drop
- Tips: The map itself has dangerous mods. Unique bosses have high damage output.

**Atziri, Queen of the Vaal**:
- Normal Atziri: Access via Sacrifice fragments (from Vaal side areas). Moderate difficulty.
- Uber Atziri: Access via Mortal fragments (dropped by normal Atziri). Hard. Famous for reflect — one of her phases reflects all damage, so don't attack during it.
- Drops: Atziri's Promise (normal), Atziri's Acuity/Disfavour (uber)

### Tier 3: Uber Bosses (Pinnacle of Pinnacle)

Uber versions of the 7 main pinnacle bosses. Accessed by combining 5 specific fragments per boss.
- **Uber Shaper**, **Uber Uber Elder**, **Uber Sirus**, **Uber Maven**, **Uber Exarch**, **Uber Eater**, **Uber Venarius**
- Also: **Uber Incarnation of Neglect/Fear/Dread** (3.28)
- Area Level 85, massively enhanced mechanics, additional attacks, faster patterns
- Drops: Forbidden Flame/Flesh jewels, uber-exclusive uniques, extremely valuable crafting materials
- **These are designed for fully optimized characters.** Most players never fight them. No shame in skipping.

## Core League Mechanics (Available in Maps)

These are previous league mechanics that have been integrated into the core game. They appear in maps based on your Atlas passive tree investment.

### Breach
- Purple hands appear in maps. Walk into them to open a Breach portal.
- Breach monsters swarm from the expanding portal. Kill them for splinters and loot.
- 100 splinters of one Breach lord = 1 Breachstone (boss encounter)
- Breach Lords: Esh, Tul, Xoph, Uul-Netol, Chayula (hardest/most valuable)
- Reworked in 3.28 with new rewards and mechanics.

### Legion
- Monoliths in maps. Activate to freeze two opposing armies in time.
- Damage frozen enemies within the time limit to "release" them for loot.
- Releases give splinters for Timeless Emblems (Domain of Timeless Conflict encounters).
- Good source of currency, Timeless Jewels, and incubators.

### Delirium
- Delirium mirror appears in maps. Walking through it starts a fog that spreads across the map.
- The deeper into the fog you go (or the longer you stay), the harder monsters get and the better rewards become.
- Delirium Orbs can be applied to maps to add permanent Delirium layers.
- Source of Cluster Jewels and Simulacrum Splinters.

### Simulacrum
- Endgame wave encounter (30 waves). Accessed via Simulacrum Splinters (from Delirium).
- Progressively harder waves with increasingly good rewards.
- Waves 25-30 are extremely difficult but drop valuable loot.
- Build check: If you can't clear wave 20+, your build isn't ready for this content.

### Harvest
- Harvest groves appear in maps. Kill monsters for Lifeforce.
- Lifeforce is spent on crafting options (see crafting.md).
- One of the most important sources of deterministic crafting.

### Delve (Azurite Mine)
- Infinite dungeon that goes deeper and deeper. Accessed via Niko + Sulphite.
- Difficulty scales infinitely with depth — eventually everything one-shots you.
- Source of Fossils, Resonators, and exclusive Delve mods on items.
- Deep Delving (depth 300+) is a niche endgame for specialized builds.

### Expedition
- Excavation sites in maps. Place explosives to unearth monsters and chests.
- Logbook maps: Larger expedition encounters with specific factions.
- Source of reroll currency (Exotic Coinage, Astragali, etc.) and Expedition-specific uniques.

### Ritual
- Ritual altars in maps. Kill monsters in rituals to earn Tribute.
- Spend Tribute on items offered in the Ritual shop. Deferred items can appear later.
- Can occasionally offer extremely valuable items (including uniques worth many Divines).

### Heist
- Rogue's Harbour — plan and execute heists/grand heists with NPC rogues.
- Unique reward rooms contain specific loot types (currency, gems, enchanted items).
- Source of alternative quality gems, enchanted items, and Replica unique items.
- Reworked in 3.28 with some quality of life improvements.

### Blight
- Tower defense encounters in maps. Build towers to defend the Blight pump.
- Blighted Maps: Full maps that are entirely Blight encounters.
- Source of Oils (for anointments) and Blighted unique items.

### Betrayal (Jun's Syndicate)
- Syndicate encounters in maps. Manage Syndicate members across 4 divisions.
- Each member in each division provides different rewards when you raid their safehouse.
- Key rewards: Aisling T4 (Research) for veiled mod crafting, Hillock (Fortification) for quality crafting.
- Understanding Syndicate board management is an entire sub-system.

## Build Readiness Checks

### Can I Do This Content? Minimum Thresholds

**White Maps (T1-5):**
- 3500+ life, capped ele res (75%), basic flask setup
- Any 4-link main skill
- This should be comfortable for any functioning build

**Yellow Maps (T6-10):**
- 4000+ life, capped ele res, some chaos res (>0%)
- 5-link preferred, merciless lab done
- DPS: Should kill rare monsters in <5 seconds

**Red Maps (T11-16):**
- 4500+ life (softcore), 5500+ (hardcore)
- Capped ele res, 30%+ chaos res
- 6-link main skill, eternal lab done
- Layered defenses (not just life — need armour/evasion/block/suppression)
- DPS: Should kill map bosses in <30 seconds
- Flask setup complete with ailment removal

**Pinnacle Bosses (Exarch, Eater, Shaper, Elder):**
- 5000+ life, all res capped, proper defensive layers
- Single-target DPS: 1M+ for comfortable kills, 500K minimum for slow but safe kills
- MUST understand boss mechanics — watch a video guide first
- Bring appropriate flasks (Ruby for Exarch, chaos res for Eater)
- Stun immunity strongly recommended

**Maven:**
- 5500+ life, excellent defensive layers
- Single-target DPS: 2M+ recommended
- Memory game: Must be able to move freely while avoiding other mechanics
- Arena management: Gravity wells persist — you need a plan

**Uber Bosses:**
- 6000+ life OR equivalent ES/ward with layered defenses
- Single-target DPS: 5M+ minimum, 10M+ comfortable
- Complete mechanical knowledge of the base fight
- Fully optimized gear, flasks, jewels
- This is endgame min-max territory — expect to invest 50+ Divines in gear

**Simulacrum (Wave 25+):**
- Enough DPS to kill waves quickly before they overwhelm you
- Enough defense to survive hits from boosted monsters
- Movement speed to reposition
- Generally requires a well-rounded T16 mapper build at minimum

**Nightmare Maps (T17+):**
- Read EVERY mod before entering — some combinations are lethal
- Requires Voidstones socketed
- Generally harder than individual pinnacle bosses
- Extreme modifier stacking can create very dangerous environments

## Endgame Progression Roadmap

### Phase 1: Atlas Completion (Level 68-80)
1. Complete white maps, expand toward all four quadrants
2. Focus one quadrant first for faster Voidstone access
3. Alch and go — don't over-invest in maps yet
4. Complete Merciless Lab for 6th ascendancy point
5. Start Maven witnessing (blue button on Atlas)
6. Begin Exarch/Eater influence progression (red/green buttons)

### Phase 2: First Voidstone (Level 80-85)
1. Push toward first Voidstone (typically Exarch/Eater quadrant)
2. Fight Black Star and Infinite Hunger as they appear
3. First Searing Exarch and Eater of Worlds fights
4. Socket first Voidstone — maps in that quadrant now drop at higher tiers
5. Complete Eternal Lab for final ascendancy points
6. Start using Scarabs on red maps

### Phase 3: All Voidstones (Level 85-90)
1. Progress all four quadrants toward their Voidstones
2. Fight Shaper and Elder (collect Guardian fragments)
3. Complete Maven's questline and fight Maven
4. Progress Eagon questline and fight Incarnations
5. Socket all 4 Voidstones — all maps can now be T16
6. Begin Atlas passive specialization for farming

### Phase 4: Endgame Farming (Level 90+)
1. Specialize Atlas tree for your preferred farming strategy
2. Use Astrolabes to create Shaped Regions for targeted farming
3. Complete Memory Vaults for powerful rewards
4. Farm Nightmare Maps for Atlas points + valuable drops
5. Run pinnacle bosses for profit (Exarch/Eater invitations, Maven writs)
6. Optional: Attempt Uber bosses with fully optimized gear

### Phase 5: Min-Max (Level 95+)
1. Uber boss farming for Forbidden Flesh/Flame, uber-exclusive drops
2. Deep Delve for challenge and niche rewards
3. Simulacrum wave 30 completion
4. Perfect gear through advanced crafting
5. Challenge completion for cosmetic rewards
6. This phase is optional — most players are happily farming at Phase 4

## Practical Decision Framework

### What to Farm
- **Currency**: High-quantity maps with Breach/Legion/Delirium Scarabs + corresponding Atlas passives
- **Crafting materials**: Harvest + Expedition for Lifeforce and reroll currency
- **Boss drops**: Pinnacle boss farming (Exarch/Eater cycle is the most accessible)
- **Specific uniques**: Farm the boss that drops what you need (check wiki for drop tables)
- **XP**: Dense maps with safe mods. Avoid rippy mods if you care about not dying.

### When Your Build Hits a Wall
1. **Dying in T11+ maps?** → Check defenses. Are you missing a layer? (See defenses.md and diagnostics.md)
2. **Damage too low for bosses?** → Check DPS in PoB. Are your supports optimal? Gem levels up? (See build-patterns.md)
3. **Can't sustain maps?** → Check Atlas passives. Are you investing in map sustain nodes?
4. **Bosses one-shot you?** → Learn the fight mechanics. Most boss deaths are mechanical, not gear-based.
5. **Bored of mapping?** → Switch content types. Try Delve, Heist, or boss farming for variety.
