# Gear Slots & Mod Priorities — Complete Reference

## Gear Slot Overview

Every character has 10 equipment slots (plus 5 flask slots covered in flasks.md). Each slot has different mod pools and serves different functions in a build.

### The Slots
1. **Weapon** (Main Hand) — Primary damage source for attacks; +gem levels for spells
2. **Off Hand** (Shield / Dual Wield / Quiver / none for 2H) — Defense or secondary offense
3. **Helmet** — Defense + utility mods (reservation efficiency, +gem levels, lab enchant)
4. **Body Armour** — Largest life/ES pool, 6-link main skill
5. **Gloves** — Defense + attack/cast speed + utility (Eldritch implicits matter)
6. **Boots** — Movement speed (mandatory) + defense + utility
7. **Belt** — Life + resistances + flask mods (Stygian Vise for Abyss jewel)
8. **Amulet** — Offensive stats + anointment + attributes
9. **Ring 1** — Resistances + life + offensive mods
10. **Ring 2** — Same as Ring 1

---

## Universal Priority Rules (Apply to ALL Builds)

Before looking at build-specific mods, every slot should address these in order:

1. **Elemental Resistances**: Cap at 75% after -60% penalty = need 135%+ total from gear. This comes from suffixes on nearly every slot.
2. **Life / Energy Shield**: Every slot should contribute to your health pool. Life is a prefix. ES can be prefix or base implicit.
3. **Attributes**: Ensure you meet gem and gear requirements (Str, Dex, Int). Amulet and belt are the best slots for attribute fixing.
4. **Chaos Resistance**: Aim for 0% minimum, 30%+ for comfortable endgame. Lower priority than ele res but still important.
5. **Movement Speed** (boots only): 25-30% is standard. Never sacrifice MS on boots — it's both offense (faster mapping) and defense (dodging).

Once survival needs are met, remaining affixes go to offense.

## Slot-by-Slot Breakdown

### Weapon (Main Hand)

**For Attack Builds:**
- **#1 Priority**: Raw weapon DPS (physical DPS for phys builds, elemental DPS for ele builds)
- High base attack speed × high flat damage = pDPS/eDPS
- Local increased physical damage % (prefix — this is LOCAL, multiplies the weapon's own damage)
- Flat added physical damage to attacks (prefix)
- Increased attack speed (suffix — also LOCAL on weapons)
- Critical strike chance (suffix — LOCAL on weapons, multiplies weapon base crit)
- Shopping tip: Search by pDPS or eDPS on trade site. A 400+ pDPS weapon in early maps is solid; 500+ is great; 600+ is endgame.

**For Spell Builds:**
- **#1 Priority**: +1 to Level of All [Element/Type] Spell Skill Gems
- +1 to Level of All Spell Skill Gems (generic, stacks with element-specific)
- Spell damage % / Elemental damage %
- Cast speed
- Crit chance for spells / Crit multiplier
- Trigger socketed spells craft (crafting bench — automates utility skills)
- Shopping tip: A "+1 fire, +1 all spell" sceptre gives +2 to fire spell gems. Combined with Empower, this is massive.

**For Minion Builds:**
- +1 to Level of All Minion Skill Gems
- Minion damage %
- Trigger socketed spells craft (for Desecrate + Offering automation)
- Minion attack/cast speed
- Often use a Convoking Wand (minion-specific base with minion mod weighting)

**For DoT Builds:**
- +1 to Level of All [Element/Chaos] Spell Skill Gems
- Damage over Time Multiplier (suffix — Hunter influence or crafted)
- +1 to Level of All Spell Skill Gems
- Spell damage %
- Non-ailment Chaos DoT Multi (for chaos DoT specifically)

### Off Hand (Shield / Dual Wield / Quiver)

**Shield** (most common defensive choice):
- Life (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix)
- % increased Maximum Life (shaper/hunter — endgame)
- Spell suppression chance (evasion bases)
- Block chance (implicit on most shields)
- +1 to Level of All [Type] Spell Skill Gems (some bases)
- Energy Shield (ES-based builds)
- Shopping tip: A shield with life + res + spell suppression or block is excellent for most builds.

**Dual Wield** (second weapon):
- Same priorities as main hand weapon
- Builds alternate attacks between hands — both weapons contribute
- Stat sticks (weapons that grant bonuses without being used for attacks) were nerfed but some niche uses remain

**Quiver** (bow builds only):
- Life
- Flat added damage (flat phys, flat ele — huge for bows)
- Crit chance / Crit multiplier
- Attack speed
- Elemental resistances
- Projectile speed (quality of life)

### Helmet

**Universal Mods:**
- Life / Energy Shield (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix)
- +X to Level of Socketed [Type] Gems (for utility gems socketed here)
- % increased Mana Reservation Efficiency of Skills (suffix — Deafening Essence of Loathing guarantees this)

**Eldritch Implicits (Helmets benefit hugely):**
- Searing Exarch: Mana reservation efficiency (extremely valuable for aura builds)
- Eater of Worlds: +level to specific gem types

**Lab Enchantments:**
- Skill-specific enchants from the Eternal Labyrinth
- Examples: "+40% Cyclone damage," "+2 Tornado Shot projectiles," "+12% Absolution damage"
- Some enchants are build-defining (Tornado Shot +2 proj is mandatory for TS builds)
- Can be expensive to acquire — check trade site for enchanted helmets

**Build-Specific:**
- Minion builds: Bone Helmet base (+minion damage implicit), +minion gem levels
- ES builds: Hubris Circlet base (highest ES)
- Armour builds: Royal Burgonet or similar high-armour base

### Body Armour

**The Most Important Gear Slot** — this is where your 6-link main skill lives.

**Universal Mods:**
- Life / Energy Shield (prefix — body armour has the HIGHEST flat life/ES rolls)
- Elemental resistances (suffix)
- Base armour/evasion/ES from the item type itself matters (pick base matching your defense type)
- Socket colors: Body armour base determines socket color weighting (Str base = red, Dex = green, Int = blue)

**Key Prefixes:**
- +# to Maximum Life (T1 requires ilvl 86 — up to +120)
- % increased Maximum Life (Hunter influence — stacks multiplicatively with flat life)
- +# to Maximum Energy Shield
- % increased Armour/Evasion/ES (local — scales the base)
- +1 to Level of Socketed Gems (corruption implicit)
- +2 to Level of Socketed [Duration/AoE/etc.] Gems (corruption or influence)

**Key Suffixes:**
- Elemental resistances
- % chance to Suppress Spell Damage (evasion bases)
- Frenzy Charge on Hit (Redeemer influence — very expensive, very powerful)
- Additional Curse (Hunter influence — enables multi-curse builds)
- Critical Strike Chance (attacks or spells)

**Unique Body Armours Worth Knowing:**
- **Tabula Rasa**: 6 white linked sockets, no stats. THE budget 6-link for leveling/early maps.
- **Skin of the Loyal**: 6 linked sockets, +1 to all socketed gems, random socket colors. Good for spell/minion builds.
- **The Brass Dome**: Massive armour, +max ele res, take no extra crit damage. Tanky choice.
- **Doryani's Prototype**: Lightning res overrides. Build-defining for lightning builds.
- **Shavronne's Wrappings**: Chaos damage doesn't bypass ES. Mandatory for Low Life ES builds.

### Gloves

**Universal Mods:**
- Life / Energy Shield (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix)
- Attack speed / Cast speed (suffix)
- Accuracy rating (suffix — attacks only)

**Eldritch Implicits (very impactful on gloves):**
- Searing Exarch: Unnerve on hit (enemies take 10% increased spell damage)
- Eater of Worlds: Fire/Cold/Lightning Exposure on hit (-exposure to enemy res)
- These implicits can replace gem socket utility — very efficient

**Build-Specific:**
- Attack builds: Flat added damage to attacks, attack speed
- Spell builds: +level to socketed gems, cast speed
- DoT builds: DoT multiplier (Hunter/Elder influence)
- Minion builds: Minion damage, minion attack speed
- Some builds use unique gloves for specific mechanics

### Boots

**The Movement Speed Slot** — 25-30% MS is non-negotiable.

**Universal Mods:**
- **Movement speed** (prefix — 25% crafted minimum, 30%+ rolled, 35% veiled). NEVER skip this.
- Life / Energy Shield (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix)
- Spell suppression (suffix — evasion bases)

**Eldritch Implicits:**
- Searing Exarch: Action speed (additive with Tailwind/Onslaught), movement speed
- Eater of Worlds: Cooldown recovery rate (crucial for CoC builds), ailment avoidance

**Influence Mods:**
- Tailwind (Redeemer) — 8% increased action speed. Build-defining for many builds.
- Elusive on crit (Hunter) — dodge + movement speed on crit
- Cooldown recovery rate (Shaper) — important for CoC and CWDT builds
- Cannot be Frozen (craft or suffix) — frees up a flask suffix

**Unique Boots Worth Knowing:**
- **Atziri's Step**: Life, evasion, spell dodge. Solid budget option.
- **Darkray Vectors**: +1 max frenzy charges. For frenzy stackers.
- **Sin Trek**: High ES, Int, Dex. Good for ES builds.

### Belt

**Universal Mods:**
- Life (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix — belts are a key res source)
- Strength (suffix — helps meet requirements AND gives life per 2 Str)
- Chaos resistance (suffix)
- Flask mods: Increased flask effect duration, increased flask charges gained

**Base Type Matters:**
- **Stygian Vise**: Has an Abyss socket. Best rare belt base for virtually every build.
- **Leather Belt**: Implicit +40 life. Good if Stygian not available.
- **Heavy Belt**: Implicit +Strength. Good for Str-stacking.
- **Crystal Belt**: Implicit +ES. For ES builds.
- **Rustic Sash**: Implicit % increased Physical Damage. For phys attack builds.

**Unique Belts Worth Knowing:**
- **Headhunter**: Steal rare monster mods on kill. THE chase unique for mapping. Absurdly expensive.
- **Mageblood**: 4 flask effects are permanent. Arguably the most powerful unique in the game. Extremely expensive.
- **Darkness Enthroned**: TWO Abyss sockets with 75% increased effect. For Abyss jewel stacking.

### Amulet

**The Offensive Jewelry Slot** — amulets have the best offensive mod options + anointment.

**Universal Mods:**
- Life / Energy Shield (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix — though amulet res competes with offensive suffixes)
- Attributes (any — amulet is the #1 slot for fixing attribute requirements)

**Offensive Mods (what makes amulets special):**
- +1 to Level of All Skill Gems (prefix — extremely rare and expensive, massively powerful)
- +1 to Level of All [Element/Type] Skill Gems (prefix — more common, still very strong)
- Critical Strike Multiplier (suffix — amulets roll high crit multi)
- Damage penetrates X% elemental resistance (suffix)
- Flat added damage to attacks or spells

**Anointment:** Apply 3 Oils for a free tree Notable. Always anoint. See passive-tree.md.

**Catalysts:** Apply AFTER crafting is complete (removed on reroll).

**Unique Amulets Worth Knowing:**
- **Astramentis**: All attributes. For attribute-stacking or fixing requirements.
- **Ashes of the Stars**: +1 all gem levels, +quality, reservation efficiency. Extremely strong and expensive.
- **Badge of the Brotherhood**: Max frenzy = max power charges. For charge stackers.

### Rings

**The Resistance Fixers** — rings shore up missing resistances and add utility.

**Universal Mods:**
- Life (prefix)
- Elemental resistances (suffix — primary res-capping slot)
- Attributes (suffix)

**Offensive Mods:**
- Flat added damage to attacks (prefix)
- Critical Strike Multiplier (suffix)
- Accuracy rating (suffix)
- Minimum charges (suffix — expensive)
- Curse on Hit (influenced)

**Base Types Matter:**
- **Opal Ring**: % increased Elemental Damage. Best for ele builds.
- **Steel Ring**: Flat physical to attacks. Best for phys attacks.
- **Diamond Ring**: % increased Global Crit. For crit builds.
- **Amethyst Ring**: +Chaos Res. Helps with chaos gearing.
- **Two-Stone Ring**: Two resistance implicits. Best for res capping.
- **Vermillion Ring**: % increased Maximum Life. Best for life builds.
- **Unset Ring**: Has a socket. Enables one more gem.

**Unique Rings Worth Knowing:**
- **Mark of the Shaper / Elder**: Set bonus for huge spell damage when paired.
- **Essence Worm**: No reservation for socketed aura, +40% all other reservation. For single-aura builds.
- **Storm Secret**: HoT triggers on shock. Build-defining for HoT autobombers.
- **Circle of [Element]**: Herald-scaling rings.

## Gear Shopping Guide

### Budget Gearing Priority Order
When currency is limited, upgrade in this order:

1. **Weapon**: Biggest DPS upgrade per chaos (attacks) or +gem levels (spells)
2. **Body Armour**: 6-link is a massive power spike. Even Tabula Rasa counts.
3. **Belt** (Stygian Vise + Abyss jewel): Cheap to get a good one
4. **Boots**: Movement speed + life + res. Easy to find cheaply.
5. **Helmet**: Life + res + reservation efficiency
6. **Rings**: Resistance capping. Two-Stone or resist-heavy rings.
7. **Amulet**: Offensive upgrade once res is covered. DON'T FORGET TO ANOINT.
8. **Gloves**: Life + res + attack/cast speed. Last to upgrade usually.

### Gear Check Red Flags
If any are true, fix immediately:

- [ ] Any elemental resistance below 75% (after penalty)
- [ ] Less than 4000 life in maps
- [ ] Chaos resistance below -30%
- [ ] Main skill not in a 5-link by yellow maps
- [ ] No movement speed on boots
- [ ] Empty amulet anointment
- [ ] Empty jewel sockets
- [ ] No Stygian Vise belt
- [ ] Missing ailment immunity on flasks

## Archetype-Specific Gear Priority Summary

### Physical Attack (Champion/Slayer)
Weapon: pDPS > flat phys > attack speed. Body: Life + armour + res. Belt: Stygian + life + res. Amulet: Crit multi + flat phys + anoint. Rings: Steel Ring, life + res + flat phys.

### Elemental Spell Crit (Inquisitor/Assassin)
Weapon: +gem levels > spell damage > crit. Body: Life/ES + res + 6-link. Belt: Life + res + attributes. Amulet: +gem levels + crit multi + anoint (penetration). Rings: Opal Ring, life + res + crit multi.

### Chaos DoT (Occultist/Trickster)
Weapon: +chaos gem levels > DoT multi > spell damage. Body: Life/ES + res. Belt: Life + res + chaos res. Amulet: +gem levels + DoT multi + anoint. Rings: Life + res + chaos res.

### Minion (Necromancer)
Weapon: Convoking Wand, +minion gem levels, trigger craft. Body: Life + res + block. Belt: Life + res + strength. Amulet: +minion/all gem levels + anoint. Rings: Life + res.

### Bow (Deadeye/Raider)
Weapon: Bow eDPS or pDPS + crit. Off Hand: Quiver with life + flat damage + crit. Body: Evasion + suppress + 6-link. Boots: MS + Tailwind. Amulet: Crit multi + flat damage + anoint.

### RF / DoT Caster (Inquisitor/Juggernaut)
Weapon: +fire/spell gem levels + fire damage. Shield: Life + res + +max fire res. Body: Life + armour + res. Amulet: +gem levels + life regen + anoint. Boots: MS + life + res.

## Practical Decision Framework

1. **Always cap resistances first.** Nothing else matters if you die to elemental damage.
2. **Life on every piece of gear.** Prefix slot dedicated to life on every item.
3. **Weapon/6-link are your biggest DPS upgrades.** Prioritize these for offense.
4. **Don't neglect utility**: Movement speed (boots), reservation efficiency (helmet), anointment (amulet), Abyss jewel (belt).
5. **Check Eldritch implicits on helmet/gloves/boots** — free stats from Exarch/Eater currency.
6. **Upgrade incrementally** — a 10c improvement now beats saving for a 5 divine improvement later.
7. **Run the gear check red flags list** every time you feel stuck.
