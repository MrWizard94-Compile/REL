# Bandits & Pantheon — Complete Reference

## Deal with the Bandits (Act 2)

In Act 2, you must choose to help one of three bandit lords or kill all three. This choice is PERMANENT (can be changed later with a vendor recipe, but it costs an Orb of Regret + Book of Reform).

### The Four Options

**Kill All Three (Help Eramir)** — +2 Passive Skill Points
- The DEFAULT choice for the vast majority of builds
- 2 passive points is almost always more valuable than any single bandit bonus
- Those 2 points can be life, damage, jewel socket, or anything your build needs
- **When in doubt, kill all three.**

**Help Oak** — +1% Life Regenerated per second, 2% additional Physical Damage Reduction, 20% increased Physical Damage
- Niche use: RF (Righteous Fire) builds that desperately need regen
- The phys damage reduction and regen are decent for tanky physical builds
- Rarely chosen — 2 passive points usually provide more total value even for builds that want regen
- Consider for: Juggernaut RF, some very tanky Marauder builds

**Help Kraityn** — 6% increased Attack and Cast Speed, 3% chance to Dodge Attacks, 6% increased Movement Speed
- The speed package — useful for builds that heavily value speed
- The dodge is minor (3% is negligible)
- Movement speed is nice quality of life but not build-defining
- Consider for: Raider builds, speed-focused mappers, some bow builds
- Still usually worse than 2 passive points

**Help Alira** — +5 Mana Regenerated per second, +20% to Global Critical Strike Multiplier, +15% to all Elemental Resistances
- The most situationally competitive option vs killing all
- +20% crit multi is a genuine DPS gain for crit builds
- +15% all ele res helps with gearing (saves suffix slots on gear)
- +5 mana regen is minor but not nothing
- Consider for: Crit builds that are tight on resistances, especially in early league when gearing is hard
- **Best alternative to killing all** — if any bandit is worth helping, it's usually Alira for crit builds

### Decision Framework

| Build Type | Best Choice | Reasoning |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Most builds | Kill All | 2 passive points > any bandit reward |
| Crit builds (early league) | Alira or Kill All | Crit multi + res is competitive with 2 points |
| RF / Regen tank | Oak or Kill All | Regen helps, but 2 points usually still better |
| Speed mapper | Kill All | Kraityn's speed is nice but not worth 2 points |
| Minion builds | Kill All | No bandit reward helps minions |
| DoT builds (non-crit) | Kill All | No bandit reward is relevant |

### Changing Your Bandit Choice
- Vendor recipe: Book of Reform (specific to desired choice) + Orb of Regret
- Books of Reform drop from specific Act 2 content
- This means bandit choice isn't truly permanent — but it costs currency to change
- If you're leveling a new character, just make the right choice upfront

## Pantheon System

The Pantheon provides passive bonuses from captured gods. You gain access by defeating Act bosses. Each Pantheon power can be upgraded by capturing specific map bosses using Divine Vessels.

### How It Works
- **Major Pantheon**: Choose ONE major god power (from 4 options). Provides a significant defensive bonus.
- **Minor Pantheon**: Choose ONE minor god power (from 7 options). Provides a secondary defensive bonus.
- You can swap between Pantheon choices freely in town — no cost
- Each power can be UPGRADED by placing a Divine Vessel in the map device with a specific map, killing the boss, and capturing their soul
- Upgraded powers add additional bonuses on top of the base effect

### Major Pantheon Powers

**Soul of the Brine King** (Act 6 boss)
- Base: Cannot be Stunned if you've been Stunned or Blocked a Stunning Hit in the past 2 seconds
- Upgraded: Cannot be Frozen if you've been Frozen recently; 50% reduced Chill Effect on you
- **Best for**: Builds without stun immunity from other sources. The anti-stun-lock is crucial — without it, getting stunned once can chain into death. This is the DEFAULT choice for most builds.
- Pairs with: Life builds that don't have Unwavering Stance, Juggernaut Unstoppable, or other stun prevention

**Soul of Lunaris** (Act 8 boss)
- Base: 1% additional Physical Damage Reduction for each nearby Enemy (up to 8%). 1% increased Movement Speed for each nearby Enemy (up to 8%).
- Upgraded: Avoid projectiles that have chained. 10% chance to avoid Projectile Damage.
- **Best for**: Mapping — the phys reduction scales with pack density. Projectile avoidance helps in projectile-heavy maps.
- Pairs with: Mapping-focused builds, evasion builds that want supplemental phys reduction

**Soul of Solaris** (Act 8 boss)
- Base: 6% additional Physical Damage Reduction if there is only one nearby Enemy. 20% chance to take 50% less Area Damage from Hits.
- Upgraded: 8% reduced Elemental Damage taken if you haven't been Hit recently. Immune to Elemental Ailments while on Consecrated Ground.
- **Best for**: Boss fights — the "only one nearby enemy" condition is perfect for 1v1 boss encounters. Area damage reduction helps against boss slams.
- Pairs with: Boss-killing setups, Inquisitor (creates Consecrated Ground for ailment immunity upgrade)

**Soul of Arakaali** (Act 7 boss)
- Base: 10% reduced Damage taken from Damage Over Time. 40% increased Recovery Rate of Life and Energy Shield if you've stopped taking Damage Over Time Recently.
- Upgraded: 50% increased Recovery Rate of Energy Shield if you've stopped taking Damage Over Time Recently (separate from the life recovery bonus). +25% to Chaos Resistance against Damage Over Time.
- **Best for**: DoT-heavy content (Uber Atziri, Elder, chaos damage maps). Builds that self-damage with Blood Rage or RF (stopping BR triggers the recovery bonus).
- Pairs with: ES builds, RF builds, builds using Blood Rage

### Minor Pantheon Powers

**Soul of Gruthkul** (Act 7)
- 1% additional Physical Damage Reduction for each Hit you've taken recently (up to 5%)
- Upgraded: Enemies that have Hit you recently have 8% reduced Attack Speed
- **Best for**: General mapping. Phys reduction stacks up during combat.

**Soul of Yugul** (Act 8)
- 50% reduced Effect of Curses on you. 30% reduced Reflected Damage taken.
- Upgraded: 5% reduced Cold Damage taken if you've been Hit recently
- **Best for**: Curse-heavy content (cursed maps, specific bosses). Reflect mitigation if you occasionally hit reflect rares.

**Soul of Abberath** (Act 6)
- 60% less Duration of Ignite on you. Unaffected by Burning Ground.
- Upgraded: 10% increased Movement Speed while on Burning Ground
- **Best for**: Burning Ground maps, Atziri fights (burning ground phases). Niche but useful.

**Soul of Tukohama** (Act 6)
- While stationary, gain 2% additional Physical Damage Reduction each second (up to 8%). While stationary, gain 0.5% of Life Regenerated per second each second (up to 2%).
- Upgraded: While stationary, gain 25% chance to be Unaffected by Burning Ground each second
- **Best for**: Stationary builds (channelling Cyclone counts as stationary if you spin in place, some totem builds). The regen and phys reduction are strong if you stand still.

**Soul of Ralakesh** (Act 7)
- 25% reduced Physical Damage over Time taken. Moving while Bleeding doesn't cause you to take extra Damage.
- Upgraded: 25% chance to avoid Bleeding. Cannot be Blinded
- **Best for**: Bleed-heavy content. The "moving while bleeding" protection prevents the 50% more bleed damage from moving. Good if you don't have bleed immunity from flasks.

**Soul of Garukhan** (Act 9)
- +5% chance to Evade Attacks if you've taken a Savage Hit recently
- Upgraded: 6% increased Movement Speed if you haven't been Hit recently. 60% reduced Effect of Shock on you.
- **Best for**: Evasion builds. The shock reduction is solid if you don't have shock immunity elsewhere.

**Soul of Shakari** (Act 9)
- 5% reduced Chaos Damage taken. 25% reduced Chaos Damage over Time taken while on Caustic Ground.
- Upgraded: Immune to Poison while there are at least 3 Poisons on you. Immune to Caustic Ground.
- **Best for**: Chaos damage mitigation. The poison immunity (at 3+ stacks) and caustic ground immunity are useful in chaos-heavy encounters (Al-Hezmin, certain map mods).

## Pantheon Selection by Build Type

### For General Mapping
- **Major**: Lunaris (pack density scaling) or Brine King (stun prevention)
- **Minor**: Gruthkul (phys reduction from hits) or Garukhan (evasion builds)

### For Boss Killing
- **Major**: Solaris (single enemy phys reduction, area damage reduction)
- **Minor**: Tukohama (stationary builds) or Shakari (chaos damage bosses)

### For DoT / Chaos Content
- **Major**: Arakaali (DoT damage reduction, recovery on stopping DoT)
- **Minor**: Shakari (chaos damage reduction, poison immunity)

### For Builds Without Stun Immunity
- **Major**: Brine King (anti-stun-lock — absolutely mandatory)
- **Minor**: Whatever matches your content

### For RF / Self-Damage Builds
- **Major**: Arakaali (recovery bonus triggers when Blood Rage or RF stops)
- **Minor**: Abberath (burning ground immunity) or Tukohama (stationary regen)

## Practical Decision Framework

### Bandits
1. Default to **Kill All** (2 passive points)
2. Consider **Alira** only if: you're a crit build AND tight on resistances AND early in the league
3. Consider **Oak** only if: you're RF and need every source of regen
4. Almost never choose **Kraityn** — speed is nice but not worth 2 points

### Pantheon
1. Start with **Brine King** (major) — stun prevention is the most universally important defensive layer
2. Once you have stun immunity from gear/tree/ascendancy, switch to Lunaris (mapping) or Solaris (bossing)
3. Minor Pantheon: Gruthkul for general use, Shakari for chaos content, Ralakesh if bleed is a problem
4. **Swap freely** — switch Pantheon based on what content you're running. It costs nothing.
5. **Upgrade your Pantheon** — Divine Vessel + specific map boss. The upgrades are significant. Do this in early mapping.
